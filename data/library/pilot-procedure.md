# A common procedure for peer pilots

Working brief · 2026-10-27

Assign a site ID, location code, responsible operator and protocol version. Record current practice before introducing the agreed change, and track observations in the same units across sites.

The proposed procedure uses a 14-day baseline and aims for 28 stable observation days after an appropriate adaptation period. A named qualified reviewer must accept any animal-feeding changes. Production experiments can be evaluated separately from household feeding.

Record all inputs, water including cleaning, labour, output, discarded batches and support time. Keep withdrawals and missing data in the final report. A small initial pilot does not establish performance throughout Maharashtra or in every season.
