# Spineless cactus: sourcing and local evaluation

Working brief · 2026-10-27

Existing research identifies BAIF and agricultural institutions as leads for fodder planting material. Availability, variety, price, transport and local suitability remain questions to confirm directly.

Local trials need to document establishment, drainage, pest incidence, water and labour before relying on harvest assumptions. Benefits seen in other species or climates should not be assumed for local cattle.

This brief provides research context, not a feeding recipe. Obtain qualified local advice before introducing a new feed.
