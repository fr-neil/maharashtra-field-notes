# Micro-finance: full cost at each site

Working brief · 2026-10-27

Record setup purchases, borrowed assets, donated materials and the family’s contribution separately. Routine costs include the whole feed or crop input bill, water, power, washing, losses, transport, repairs and local support.

Show actual receipts and avoided purchases without double-counting a saving already reflected in a lower expense. Value unpaid labour separately and include routine programme support when judging reproducibility.

Keep money received separate from applications and promised subsidies. Compute payback only from a positive observed incremental cash benefit, and disclose seasonal assumptions. No financial outcomes are established yet.
