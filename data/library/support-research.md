# Nashik fodder support: questions to verify

Working brief · 2026-10-27

The workspace research identifies possible fodder conservation and livestock-support programmes, but current local availability and scheme terms need original orders and implementing-office confirmation.

For each lead, confirm geography, eligible applicant, supported activity, application route, deadlines, required contribution, reimbursement timing and responsible contact. A news announcement does not establish an open application window.

Keep the original Marathi order and amendments alongside short summaries. Record what the local office confirms and the date; do not reuse historical camp rates as current benefits.
