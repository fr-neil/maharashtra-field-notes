# How the hub treats evidence

Working brief · 2026-10-27

Official observations, statistical estimates, forecasts, peer reports and modelled scenarios are separate record types. Every displayed value needs a location, date, unit, source and method.

A reservoir’s storage percentage is not a village supply estimate. Link the actual supply system, usable allocation, withdrawals and losses before estimating a shortage date. Leave estimates unavailable when the inputs are missing.

Census records and soil samples retain their reference dates. A peer’s local report remains a peer observation after review; it does not become an official reading. Household contact and financial information belongs in restricted records.
