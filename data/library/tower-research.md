# Tower designs and experimental growing systems

Working brief · 2026-10-27

The workspace compares pocket towers, spray-cap columns and growing systems with media. An additional printable tower design includes downloaded parts, estimated material requirements and builder-reported watering and leakage issues.

The raw geometry report contains disputed measurements, so dimensional claims need reconciliation before design or manufacture. Material estimates do not represent a complete installed or operating price.

Compare candidate systems under documented local conditions using the same crop where practical. Record usable harvest, all direct water, labour, downtime, repairs and actual sale receipts.
