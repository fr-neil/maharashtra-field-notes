# Maharashtra drought resilience: project priorities

Working brief · 2026-10-27

The project aims to help farming families across Maharashtra manage water and fodder shortages through affordable practices they can maintain locally. Nashik is the starting location, with peer pilots and regional participation planned across the state.

The recorded priority is a complete, locally appropriate cattle-feeding approach, followed by safety and practical evidence for sprouted fodder. Crop-income and tower experiments remain separate candidates to evaluate with full water, labour and financial records.

Research notes contain candidate ideas and unresolved claims. They are not a finished animal-feeding protocol or evidence of a completed field trial. Qualified collaborators should review feeding changes for the animal and local ingredients.
